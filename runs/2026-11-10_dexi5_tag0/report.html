Setup

  • Aircraft: DEXI-5 (ARK Pi6X + CM4), downward rangefinder and optical flow, no GPS, magnetometer off.
  • Power: external power supply set to 24.0 V, tethered to the aircraft (about 23.9 V at the flight controller under load). No battery is fitted, so the voltage stays flat and the power figure is what holding a hover costs.
  • Hold: the pilot flips CH7 to Offboard; tag_nav centers on tag 0, turns the nose to the tag's top edge, and holds, correcting from every detection.
  • Height: rangefinder is the EKF height reference with the barometer as a backup (EKF2_HGT_REF 2, EKF2_BARO_CTRL 1). With the barometer off, the EKF reset its height every 5 s and the aircraft bobbed 0.2–0.3 m.
  • Heading: the EKF heading drifts slowly without a magnetometer; the tag holds the real heading, so EKF heading drift here is not aircraft rotation.
  • Logging: 1 Hz on the CM4, one summary per minute. Tag error is the camera-measured distance from the frame center to the tag center.
